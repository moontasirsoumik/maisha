# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Architecture studios, recruiters, and collaborators reviewing Maisha Mahpara's portfolio on desktop and mobile. They need to understand her design range quickly, then inspect selected work in enough depth to judge authorship, process, and craft.

## Product Purpose

Present Maisha Mahpara as an architecture graduate with a thoughtful, research-led design practice. Success means a visitor can scan the portfolio, open a case study, understand the project premise, and contact Maisha without friction.

## Brand Personality

Precise, thoughtful, quietly bold. The experience should feel like a well-made architectural folio translated into a responsive digital space.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, template card grids, purple gradients, decorative glassmorphism, excessive rounded UI, and motion that distracts from the work.

## Design Principles

- Let the work lead; use interface elements as a measured frame.
- Show design thinking, not only final renders.
- Use architectural rhythm: grid, sequence, proportion, pause.
- Make every interaction legible and accessible.
- Prefer authored detail over decorative complexity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, meaningful alt text, readable contrast, touch-safe controls, and a reduced-motion mode.
