# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Studio leads, collaborators, and schools reviewing Maisha Mahpara’s architectural work. They need to see the drawings, the place, and what she took on.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio of eight architecture projects, plus the record of practice, training, and writing. Success is a visitor who can open a project, read the drawings, and write.

## Positioning

The drawings lead. Places are named as they are: Rangamati, Khulshi, Rajshahi, Dhaka. The site does not pitch a practice beyond the work on the page.

## Operating Context

Read on a laptop or a phone, often from a link in an email or a portfolio review. The one action is writing to mahparamaisha0@gmail.com.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Routes stay: `/`, `/about`, `/work/:slug`. Each project keeps its plates, captions, chapters, and the Rajshahi film, which plays on demand, paused and muted, with `preload="none"`. Plates open in a lightbox. Cloudflare Pages file budget and the plate size caps in `scripts/check-budget.mjs` stay in force. No invented statistics, quotes, or clients.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Maisha Mahpara. Role: architectural designer, Dhaka. Voice stays short and factual. The site is a clean gallery hang: limewash paper, umber ink, a light serif over a quiet grotesque, one slate signal, and her own drawings at large scale. The plotter roll, the near-black film, title blocks, folk motifs, and textile ornaments are out.

## Evidence on Hand

Project plates and the portrait under `public/work/`. Copy in `src/content/site.ts` and `src/content/projects.ts`. LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/maisha-mahpara. Email: mahparamaisha0@gmail.com.

## Product Principles

Show the drawing before the sentence. Keep claims inside the supplied record. One action, labeled Write. Motion serves the work, and reduced motion still reaches every project.
