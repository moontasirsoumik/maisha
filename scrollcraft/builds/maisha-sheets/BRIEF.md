# Maisha sheets

Self-authored from the locked landing choice "Two sheets, no blank". The earlier desk brief is superseded for the landing. Project pages and the record were left alone.

## Eight topics

1. Vibe: clean, cinematic, professional, modern. The user rejected the empty cream column beside one framed drawing.
2. Sequence, in their words: an introduction plus some project work. Neither a full introduction nor a full project.
3. Energy: a filled first screen, then a quiet drift inside the drawing, then the carousel as the intense pass, then a quiet close.
4. Feeling: recognition at the name and the places, curiosity as the drawing moves, range across the work, commitment at Write.
5. The thing this page does: two sheets meet at a rule, both filled, and the drawing drifts while the title stays.
6. Range: editorial, on the plotter paper already used for the rest of the site.
7. Distinct scenes, not one unbroken flight.
8. Assets: the existing Cultural Roots bird's-eye and the eight real project places. Nothing generated.

## Feeling curve

- Open: composure. Name, line, eight places, and one drawing fill the first screen.
- Drift: focus. The title holds and the drawing moves under the hand.
- Range: appetite. Selected work travels sideways, title and place only.
- Close: resolve. Write holds after the rail has gone.
