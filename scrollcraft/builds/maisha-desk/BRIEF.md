# Maisha desk

Self-authored under an explicit landing brief. The visitor named the sequence, the energy, and the limits. They did not sit a second interview.

## Eight topics

1. Vibe: clean, cinematic, professional, modern. References already rejected: cream folio, folk motifs, near-black film as the landing.
2. Sequence, in their words: an introduction, then a bit of project work. Not a full introduction. Not a full project. A combination.
3. Energy: calm name, then the drawing changes under the hand, then the carousel is the intense pass, then a quiet close.
4. Feeling: recognition at the name, curiosity as the view becomes a plan, range across the work, commitment at Write.
5. The thing no other site of theirs does: scroll draws a register while one framed drawing turns from a view into its plan.
6. Range: editorial, on the plotter paper already approved for the project pages. The landing is its own design. The plotter frame stays on the projects.
7. Distinct scenes, not one unbroken flight.
8. Assets: the existing plates and the circular portrait. Nothing generated.

## Feeling curve

- Open: composure. The name and one drawing share the screen.
- Turn: focus. The same frame wipes from the lake view to the masterplan.
- Range: appetite. The work travels sideways, each peek labelled with title and place only.
- Close: resolve. Write holds after the rail has gone.

## Peak

The visitor says: the drawing turned into its plan while I was still reading her name. That moment lives in the opening act, and it has the longest hold before the carousel.

## Tell-someone

It's the site where her name and one drawing share the first screen, and the drawing becomes its plan as you scroll.
