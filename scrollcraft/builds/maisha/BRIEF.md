## 2. Sequence, in the visitor's words

The user asked for the whole website, redesigned, premium, properly done. The existing site already states the order the drawings should lead: cover, the folio of eight projects, each case study, about, contact.

Authored sequence that keeps that order and makes it a walk:

1. Name, then the thesis plate arriving as a sheet on a desk.
2. The index of eight projects, each one a labelled plate.
3. Inside a project: the question, the drawings, one plate held large, the next project.
4. The person, the practice, then a way to write.

## 3. Energy

Authored. Quiet at the name. Building through the index. Loudest when one plate is held and the section line travels through it. Quiet again in the colophon, where the ask is a line of type, not a button island.

## 4. Feeling curve

Written before the score. One line per act: the emotion, then what on screen causes it.

Home:

1. Recognition. The name is already there, large, on paper. Nothing asks them to scroll.
2. Arrival. The thesis plate slides up over the name and settles as an object with weight.
3. Curiosity. Eight plates travel sideways, each labelled with place and year, none pitching.
4. Weight. The section line locks onto the plate and travels through it. The drawing is being read, not displayed.
5. Resolve. The colophon holds. The email is a line in the plate, and it stays.

Work:

1. Orientation. Title, place, role. The visitor knows whose drawing this is before the image fills the frame.
2. Reading. One paragraph, then the plates of that chapter, in a column. The page turns.
3. Absorption. One plate pins. The section line crosses it. This is the longest hold.
4. Continuation. The next chapter, or the next project, as a labelled plate. The walk does not fade out.

About:

1. Presence. Portrait and name, side by side, no media behind the type.
2. Record. Practice, education, experience, writing, as a dense folio rather than cards.
3. Invitation. Same colophon as the home close. One label: Write.

## The peak

The one moment, as a visitor would say it to a friend: "It's the site where a section line draws itself across her drawing while you scroll."

It lives in the plate-room act, on the home page and again inside each case study. That act gets the longest span.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where you walk through an architect's drawings and a section line cuts across the one that matters.

## Authored silence

The title page has no motion until the plate arrives. That stillness is the brief, not a missed cue. The colophon does not fade. If the last screen is empty, the page is wrong.

## 5. Signature seed

Authored from the work: the thing no portfolio they have seen does is treat scroll as a section cut. A hairline, labelled like a drawing (A–A), travels through one held plate. The drawing stays still. The cut moves.

## 6. Distance from premium-minimal

Authored: editorial. Paper, folio, measure, restraint. Not brutalist, not maximalist, not playful, not retro, not a dark luxury template.

Palette is not cream-and-brass. Cold paper, blue-black ink, one lake-green accent taken from the Kaptai water in the thesis renders. Two families already on the site: Fraunces for display, Plus Jakarta Sans for text. Bricolage Grotesque is dropped so the page holds two families.

## 7. World or scenes

Authored: distinct scenes. The visitor should feel they turned a plate, not that they flew through one camera move. Chaptered editorial, not worldflight. A continuous flight would hide the cuts between drawings, and the cuts are the point.

## Score

Home. Grammar: chaptered editorial. Devices in order, never the same family twice:

| Beat | Device | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Name | flow, greet, no media | Title page. Type on paper. |
| Thesis plate | parallax planes + reveal | The sheet arrives over the name. Depth from three rates, not a pin. |
| Index | pan | Lateral travel reads as a collection. |
| Section cut | pin | The peak. The line travels. Longest span. |
| Colophon | flow, in | The close holds. No magnet, no spotlight. |

Work. Per project, not a second film:

| Beat | Device | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Title plate | reveal | Hard arrival of the first plate beside the title. |
| Chapters | flow + in | Reading. Plates in a column, captions under them. |
| Held plate | pin + section cut | The peak again, on this project's own drawing. |
| Next | pan or flow link | The walk continues. |

About. flow + in only. A record should not perform.

## Grammar bans honoured

No full-bleed scrub hero. No pinned crossfade type act. No magnetic CTA. No spotlight. No centred hero copy. Media sits in its own column or as a plate, with a caption. No `01 / 06` counters. No scroll cue. No em dash in visible copy.

The Rajshahi film is a play control inside its chapter, not a scrub act. That keeps the chaptered ban on scrub beyond one chapter, and here scrub is used zero times, because the asset is a 20 MB playback file rather than a scrub master.
